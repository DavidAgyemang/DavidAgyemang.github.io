In 2025 I studied International Human Rights, Conflict Management, and Peace Building at the Jacobs-Abbey Global Institute for Leadership Studies in the USA.

Computing Coursework

NCC Modules

Software Engineering

Integrated Attendance Management System (IAMS)

A full six-task systems project for IPMC University College, from requirements through to testing: UML use case and class diagrams, a work breakdown structure and Gantt chart, clickable UI prototypes, and 16 documented test cases.

UML · Systems Analysis · UI Prototyping · Project Planning · Testing

Essentials of Management

Amazon: Structure, Strategy & Leadership

An analysis of Amazon's management approach, applying Katz's managerial skills framework, Burns and Stalker's organisational structure model, SWOT analysis alongside Goal-Setting Theory, and Woodcock's Building Blocks with Lewin's leadership styles.

Organisational Analysis · SWOT · Leadership Theory · Case Study

Electronic Business

Amazon's E-Commerce Model

A six-part study of how Amazon runs its digital business: emerging technologies, CRM, supply chain management, marketplace revenue, security threats, and a five-year e-commerce strategy.

E-Commerce · CRM · Supply Chain · Digital Strategy

Computer Systems

QuantumTech: Systems & Networking

A scenario-based project covering hardware components, operating system functions, and software categories, plus networking (LAN, MAN and WAN, topologies and the OSI model) alongside distributed and quantum computing concepts.

Networking · OSI Model · Hardware · Distributed Computing
